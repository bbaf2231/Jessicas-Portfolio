Case Study · Customer Intelligence & Growth · Forte Bar Prep

 

No engineering background. No team. A governed signal pipeline, a content engine, and a decision framework, built entirely through AI-directed development. Here's how it actually works.

Chapter 01 · The Problem

 

As Co-Partner, Customer Intelligence & Growth Strategist at Forte Bar Prep, I own the side of the business that has to know what a failed, overwhelmed retaker is actually thinking, in their own words, before they ever fill out a form. There was no system for hearing that at scale, and no governance for making sure what we heard turned into good decisions instead of noise.

What I set out to build

A way to capture real customer language across channels, structure it into something usable, and govern how it's allowed to influence strategy, so the system stays trustworthy as it scales.

Chapter 02 · The Foundation

 

Evidence has to pass through a validation layer before it can influence a decision. The system is allowed to say "not enough data yet" instead of guessing. That single rule is what keeps everything downstream trustworthy.
