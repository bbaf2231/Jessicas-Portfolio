Evidence & scope
Investigation and ownership
Based on the final internship presentation, weekly analytical decks and Jessica Whitaker’s firsthand account. The investigation covered multiple website journeys, including signup and Buy Now. Jessica conducted the behavioral and funnel analysis, built the Excel tracking model, presented findings and measured subsequent performance. The CTO and technical team investigated the backend issues and implemented code changes.
Friction illustrations
The four interface drawings are conceptual representations of observed problems, not historical bOnline screens or a single sequential funnel.
Exhibit 1 · Initial findings
Final internship presentation, page 6: email loading of 8–14 seconds, exits observed after 5–8 seconds, Continue/confirmation button response of 23–25 seconds, and provisioning loading over three minutes (approximately 180 seconds in the source). These are summarized observations across reviewed recordings. The product add-on finding comes from the broader website investigation, including Buy Now analysis. The four areas are not one universal sequential funnel.
Exhibit 2 · Response and progression checks
Final internship presentation, pages 6 and 8: initial observations compared with later reported email loading of 1.2 seconds, Continue/SMS response of 1.2 seconds, and PIN/provisioning loading of 55 seconds. The presentation reports time reductions of 89% for email loading, 95% for Continue/SMS responses, and 69% for provisioning. These are rounded summary reductions; the email benchmark was approximately 11 seconds on average and provisioning approximately 180 seconds, while initial observations are displayed as ranges or approximations. These relative time reductions are percentages, not percentage-point changes. Page 9: Continue with Email conversion of 62.6%, 65.3%, and 66.3%; Create Account conversion of 87.0%, 92.8%, and 93.6%, across June 30–July 22, 2025. First-to-last increases are 3.7 and 6.6 percentage points. The source relates account creation to the confirmation-code step. Response timings and step-conversion series are distinct measures; the exhibit does not imply matched customers or isolated experimental effects. Step-level rates are separate from overall signup completion and paid purchases.
Technical confirmation
The CRM explanation and supporting query images were supplied by the CTO. The 4.5-to-2.5-second change was described as lookup time for email/phone entry; it is one technical measurement, not the duration of an entire spinner or signup journey.
Commercial finding
The product add-on CTA finding represents an estimated $1,000 in lost revenue over three months. The issue was subsequently fixed; post-fix revenue was not measured, and no recovered-revenue claim is made.
Reported outcomes
The final presentation reports more than 80% lower average wait times across both spinners, 43% lower early abandonment and a 7.6-percentage-point increase in overall signup completion. The spinner finding is Jessica’s analysis. These outcomes reflect a broader optimization period involving database, API, loading and interface improvements; they are not isolated effects of a single API correction. Weekly monitoring continued as loading-related issues improved and other interface or error patterns remained.
