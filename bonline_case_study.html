A UX and funnel diagnostics project where I traced sign-up drop-offs back to page load latency, partnered with the CTO on the backend fix, and tracked the before-and-after impact across four weeks.

Situation

bOnline is a multi-award-winning UK VoIP and business broadband provider for small businesses. The sign-up funnel had a quiet problem: a meaningful share of users were abandoning before they ever got an account, and no one had pinned down why.

Task

As Marketing Analytics Intern, I was asked to dig into 1,000+ Hotjar recordings and Mixpanel flow data to find where users were actually getting stuck, then turn that into something the team could act on.

Action

I built an Excel tracking system to log latency at every funnel step, isolated three severe bottlenecks, and worked directly with the CTO on a database migration and API fix. Then I re-measured every week to prove the fix actually worked.

Role Scope

Diagnostic Approach and Core Responsibilities

  • 🔍

  • 📐

Diagnostic Findings

Isolating the Early-Funnel Bottlenecks

Four points in the sign-up flow accounted for nearly all severe-impact drop-offs, identified through session-replay analysis and cross-referenced against funnel timing data.

⛔ Verification Errors

Users got stuck or confused entering codes.

🔒 Unresponsive Controls

Key buttons failed to register clicks.

👤 Idle Abandonment

Users paused for long stretches, unsure how to proceed.

⏳
Email Spinner
avg ~11s

Load time ranged 8 to 14 seconds. Drop-offs concentrated once wait exceeded 5 to 8 seconds, causing immediate exits.

🔘
Continue Button
23 to 25s

Button unresponsiveness during the confirmation flow, compounded by overlapping account-creation behavior.

🔢
PIN Provisioning
40% drop-off

Provisioning exceeded 3 minutes. Lagging and frozen states drove 40% of users to abandon at this page.

📧
Confirmation Code
24.3 min

Prior to the fix, this step averaged 24.3 minutes from send to entry, the single largest source of funnel drop-off.
