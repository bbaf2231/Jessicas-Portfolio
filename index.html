The Challenge

ServiceNow set a company goal of bringing 1,600 accounts live on Agentic AI by end of year. Exchange Clinics are hands-on sessions where customers work directly with ServiceNow AI experts on their Agentic AI workflows — part of Enterprise Field Marketing's broader customer experience motion toward that goal.

The gap: real customer insight — what an account was actually blocked on, what they asked for next — surfaced inside the clinic and stayed there. Nothing carried it to the people who could act on it: the account executive, the AI Adoption Center, the CSM. Every clinic was generating signal that never reached pipeline.

What I Built

An automated workflow that turns AI experts' post-clinic notes into routed account intelligence. After each clinic, experts enter their key notes into a tracking dashboard. The workflow identifies the central customer question, synthesizes the recommended direction and agreed next steps, and routes the draft through human review. Once approved, the account team receives a summary and a guided path linked to relevant ServiceNow resources — same day, not weeks later.

Proof It Worked — Two Live Tests

Clinic Site A

Account 1

Customer asked whether a ServiceNow AI team could spend time on-site to build a practical roadmap — still early in their AI journey. The signal moved from the clinic conversation to the account executive, who re-engaged the right team. Routed and connected to the AI Adoption Center.

Clinic Site B

Account 2

Recap reached the account team the next day — response was "impeccable timing," and they were meeting with the customer that same day. The account advanced Agentic stage progression within 13 days of the clinic.

I wouldn't attribute that movement to one follow-up alone — but it demonstrates the value of carrying customer insight beyond the clinic and making the handoff visible and actionable.

Standardizing It — Three Levers

01

Standard inputs — capture the facilitator's recap in the same format every time.

02

Handoff — a defined 24-hour review and routing process.

03

Measurement — a movement dashboard tracking whether next steps were completed and whether accounts continue agentic momentum.

Reading the Numbers Honestly

109 accounts registered, 82 attended (75%). Of 104 accounts with sufficient movement data, attendees progressed at 18% vs. 12% for registered-only — roughly 1.5x greater momentum. 14 of 79 measured attended accounts reached agentic go-live (18%). None of this claims the follow-up workflow alone caused the movement — it's presented alongside the case studies as the mechanism that made the movement visible and actionable sooner.

What This Taught Me

Marketing strategy isn't just creating demand — it's understanding where customers are in their journey, connecting each experience to a meaningful next step, and measuring what happens next so the motion can be optimized toward adoption and go-live readiness. This was my first time moving a project from academic framing into real business execution: breaking down a fuzzy problem, shaping it end-to-end, and working through ambiguity without a template to follow.
