Internship case study

Measuring the business impact of AI adoption Clinics.

ServiceNow provides a cloud platform for enterprise workflows.

Its invitation-only Agentic AI Exchange Clinics paired customer teams with AI experts to build AI workflows, resolve blockers, and plan a path to production.

The internship task

Assess what happened after the Clinics, define business impact beyond attendance, and identify what customers needed to move forward.

The capstone question

Do these clinics actually drive adoption and business impact, and what happens after the room empties?

Evidence & scope

01 · Adoption comparisonMovement means advancing in Agentic AI adoption, not going live. Measurable cohorts: 9 of 50 attendees = 18.0%; 7 of 58 registered-only accounts = 12.1% (≈1.5×). This observational comparison does not establish a causal Clinic effect.

02 · Analysis scopeAccount metadata and an Excel dashboard compared progression, stagnation, regression, and timing across adoption stage, city, and account characteristics. Stages 1–3 cover early use and sub-production; Stage 4 is Production Activity; Stage 5 is Live. The 66% Stage 1 stagnation finding did not establish the cause.

03 · Program contextTen global Clinics were completed; 82 attended accounts were recorded within the program context of 108 total accounts. Separately, 11 of 82 attended accounts went live. These are account counts, not individual attendance. Onsite leadership covered San Francisco. Definitions, analysis, and test examples follow the capstone presentation and firsthand account.

04 · Walkthrough fidelityThe walkthrough uses illustrative demo conversations, not actual client information, with a distinct sample scenario on each screen. Input, summary, email, and resource wording are preserved from the original. Copilot assisted drafting; human review preceded Power Automate/Outlook routing. The first two resource labels keep placeholder links. No messages are sent.

05 · Operational results and limitsThe manager described a few hours per week spent locating accounts and preparing follow-up, reduced to seconds by the workflow; automated steps took seconds. The ≈80% figure is an operational estimate of follow-up and internal communication time, not a formal timed comparison or an adoption result.

06 · Visual referencesThe supplied brand image and ServiceNow developer site informed the palette. Original Microsoft marks identify the applications used: Copilot, Outlook, Excel, and the Power Automate icon collection.
