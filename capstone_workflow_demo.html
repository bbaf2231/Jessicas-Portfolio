Turning Clinic Insight into Account Action

An AI-assisted workflow I built that turns a facilitator's raw clinic notes into a structured, customer-ready email, with a human reviewing every summary before it sends.

Situation

During a summer internship on ServiceNow's Customer Excellence Marketing team, I worked on the Agentic AI Exchange Clinics program: invite-only, hands-on labs that move enterprise accounts through the early stages of AI adoption, from not using agentic capabilities at all, to classic non-agentic use, to testing but not yet live.

Task

My role was to understand the end-to-end operation of the clinics and answer one question for leadership: do these clinics actually drive adoption and business impact, and what happens after the room empties?

Action

I helped drive the outreach that filled the room, built the measurement framework to track what happened to accounts afterward, then built the AI-assisted workflow below so a facilitator's raw notes could reach the account team as a reviewed, structured summary within a day, instead of relying on manual follow-up.

● The Payoff
What This Actually Produced

The workflow above is one piece of a larger question: did any of this actually move accounts forward. Here's what the data showed, and how the account teams themselves responded.

Results

Attended accounts progressed 1.5x faster toward Agentic AI go-live than registered-only accounts (18% versus 12%), and 11 of 82 attended accounts are already live. The follow-up workflow cut manual handoff time by roughly 80%, turning a process that used to take days into one that reaches the account team within 24 hours.

Takeaways

Built the measurement approach, the workflow, and the strategy from the ground up with no built-in authority to demand adoption. Earned buy-in instead by learning the business well enough to speak its language, and the system kept running on every clinic that followed the internship.
