About

My path into systems and customer intelligence did not start with software. It started in hospitality, where the quality of an experience depended on people, timing, and handoffs working together. Later work — running my own business, studying consumer behavior and analytics at USC, and building customer-intelligence systems at ServiceNow — kept returning to the same instinct: understand the system, find the disconnect, and build something that makes the people inside it more capable.

Good systems make people more capable.
